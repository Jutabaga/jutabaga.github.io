# Flowers
---


I buy fresh flowers each week.

It's a little selfish ritual

Forgoing the crackers or a can of diced tomatoes

So I have a handful of flowers

For the mantle.

They stay good for a few days;

And I chance the water and trim

Their stems like a mother but

Inevitable they fade, like old friends.

They can't fit into the bin all at once, their stalks too tall, 

So each precious flower must be disfigured.

Snapping the stems feels like breaking bones

I toss the soiled water

And watch it swirl down the sink like destruction.
