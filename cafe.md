## Writing exercise 

The cafe is well-lit but somehow still shadowy; 
customers sit at tables facing the walls, on their phones or with noses in a book, as if afraid of the world.
I order a cheesecake and orange juice, like a child, and it comes delivered pristinely on a little tray.
I feel like an outpatient (is that the right word?) being fed my dessert in a hospital bed.
Whatever experimental grunge music they have playing blares, the guitars feedbacking all over my cheesecake. 

All of a sudden, I feel a bit ill; in a second, it feels like *I* might feedback on the cake as well. 
*The hour I spent out in direct sunlight probably didn't help*, I think, *I must have heatstroke.*
